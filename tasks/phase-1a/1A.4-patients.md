# 1A.4 Guest patients

## Goal
A clinic can serve people who have no app account. `patients` holds the person per organization;
appointments point at it; `appointments.user_id` becomes optional.

## What the migration does
1. **`patients (organization_id, name, phone, user_id)`**: name 1–100 chars, phone `^\+?[0-9]{7,15}$` or NULL,
   `user_id → auth.users ON DELETE SET NULL` (deleting an account keeps the clinic's record as a guest).
   Unique `(organization_id, user_id)` for account patients. Guests are **not** unique by phone (families share
   one); `issue_token` (1A.5) dedupes them by phone + name. `private.normalize_phone()`: strips separators,
   `00` → `+`, invalid → NULL.
2. **`appointments.patient_id`** (FK `RESTRICT`, indexed, **NOT NULL**). **`appointments.user_id` nullable.**
3. **Trigger `resolve_appointment_patient`**: when `patient_id` is missing it is filled from `user_id` (one record
   per account per organization, created on first booking), so **`book_appointment` and the app are unchanged**.
   A patient from another organization, or one tied to a different account, is refused.
4. **Backfill**: one patient per (organization, booking account) from existing appointments, named from the
   profile (`'Patient'` if empty), phone normalized. Appointments whose account no longer exists (there's no
   FK on `appointments.user_id`) still get a record, without `user_id`.
5. **Read access** (`private.can_view_patient`): own record, records the account booked for, the organization's
   owner, staff of a center the patient visited, the patient's doctors, platform admins. No client writes.
6. **Guest names on existing screens**: `appointments_full.patient_name` falls back to the patient record
   (and gains `patient_id` as the last column); `get_doctor_today_appointments` (was an INNER JOIN on profiles, so
   guests would have been invisible to doctors) and `get_doctor_recent_patients` read names from `patients`.

## NULL-safety fixes (required by `user_id` becoming optional)
`x.user_id <> auth.uid()` is NULL for a guest, and `IF NOT FOUND OR NULL THEN RAISE` **doesn't raise**. Without
these fixes any signed-in user could have cancelled or read the queue position of any guest token by id:
- `cancel_appointment`, `check_in_appointment` → `IS DISTINCT FROM`
- `private.can_view_appointment` (used by `get_appointment_queue_snapshot`, `people_ahead`) → `COALESCE(…, false)`
- `create_notification_on_appointment` / `_on_cancel` skip guests (`notifications.user_id` is NOT NULL; without
  this, staff couldn't cancel a guest token)
- `save_visit_summary` raises a clear error for guests: summaries are keyed to the patient's **account**
  (`visit_summaries.patient_id → auth.users`). Supporting guests there is a follow-up.

## Decisions (safest option)
- `patient_id` is **NOT NULL** (every appointment has a person), enforced after the backfill.
- A center **with appointments can no longer move** to another organization (its patients belong to the old one).
- Account patients show the **current profile name**; guest records show the name they gave.
- `patients.organization_id` is `RESTRICT`: an organization with patients can't be deleted.
