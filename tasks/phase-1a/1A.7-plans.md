# 1A.7 Plans, subscriptions, entitlements

## Goal
What a clinic can use is decided by its plan: module flags and limits, read through one helper, enforced in
`issue_token`.

## What the migration adds
- **`plans`**: `modules text[]` (checked against `private.known_modules()`: `queue, qr_join, online_booking, sms,
  whatsapp, analytics, multi_center`), `tokens_per_day`, `max_doctors`, `max_centers` (NULL = unlimited),
  `monthly_price_pkr` (NULL = not set), `monthly_message_credits`, `is_public`, `sort_order`.
- **`org_subscriptions`**: `plan_id`, `status` (`trialing | active | past_due | cancelled`), period dates; at most one
  live (non-cancelled) row per organization.
- **`org_entitlements`** (one row per organization): effective `modules` + limits, rewritten by triggers from the
  live subscription's plan (or Free when there is none) whenever a subscription or a plan changes.
- **`private.has_module(org_id, module)`**.
- Every organization, existing and new (trigger on `organizations`), starts on **Free**.
- **`issue_token`** (same as 1A.5 plus, after the caller/input checks and before any write):
  | Check | Rule | Error |
  |---|---|---|
  | module | `walk_in` → `queue`, `qr` → `qr_join`, `online`/`app` → `online_booking` | `PT402` |
  | doctors | only the organization's first `max_doctors` **active** doctors (by creation) can get tokens | `PT402` |
  | tokens/day | organization's **non-cancelled** appointments today (any source, clinic time zone) < `tokens_per_day`; advisory lock per org/day | `PT402` |

## Seeded plans
| Plan | Price (PKR/mo) | Modules | Tokens/day | Doctors | Centers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| free | 0 | queue, qr_join | 30 | 1 | 1 |
| clinic | *not set* | + online_booking, sms | 150 | 3 | 1 |
| pro | *not set* | + whatsapp, analytics | ∞ | 10 | 1 |
| center | *not set* | all, incl. multi_center | ∞ | ∞ | ∞ |

**Only Free's numbers come from the brief.** Paid prices, limits and module mixes are placeholders: set them by
hand before selling (an `UPDATE plans` updates every organization on that plan).

## Decisions (safest option)
- **Existing (default) organizations are on Free**, not grandfathered onto a paid plan: least privilege, and nothing
  existing is limited because **`book_appointment` is not plan-checked in 1A** (only the new `issue_token`), so
  the Android app behaves exactly as before. Upgrading the real clinics is a business decision (manual step).
- **The daily limit counts all of the day's non-cancelled appointments**, app bookings included (one queue).
  Cancelled ones don't count, so staff can free places taken by spam QR joins.
- **The limit applies to staff too** (it's the plan's limit, not an abuse limit).
- **The doctor limit picks the first N active doctors**, instead of blocking the admin app from adding doctors
  (that would break an existing flow).
- **Clients can't write** plans, subscriptions or entitlements. Billing and upgrades are server-side or platform
  admin (Phase 1B/1C). Members read their entitlements (the app can show what's on); only owners read
  subscriptions. Public plans are readable by anyone (pricing page).
- `max_centers` is stored but not enforced yet (no client can create centers; platform admin only).
